# First video: on-screen captions

Announces release 1.31.0. The footage is the real call of the session that makes the video.
One caption at a time, at the bottom. Every caption is 52 characters or fewer.

## Hook

The caption of the first three seconds, over the start of scene A, before caption A1.

| # | English | Français |
|---|---------|----------|
| H1 | The agents in this call are making this video | Les agents de cet appel font cette vidéo |
| H2 | Your coding agents, shown as a live team call | Vos agents de code, en appel d’équipe en direct |
| H3 | Three agents made this video. Watch them work | Trois agents ont fait cette vidéo. Regardez-les |

Pick: **H2**, after the critic. A stranger must read what the app is before anything else. H1 was the first pick; the joke now comes last, as the two captions of the recording.
