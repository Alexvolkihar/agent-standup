# Agent Standup: YouTube channel description

Drafted by the second session of the video, then revised after the critic's remarks (`critique.md`): "shows", not "turns into"; Margot introduced as the main agent; the reason before the claims; one disclaimer sentence for the channel and the video. YouTube allows 1,000 characters; each version below is under 800.

## English

```text
Agent Standup shows your Claude Code, Codex and Mistral Vibe sessions as a live team call. Free and open source.

Margot, the main agent, leads the call. Sub-agents join when they get a task, share their screen with the real output of each command, and report back out loud. You see who is working, who is waiting on whom, and who needs your answer, across all your sessions.

It only reads the session files these tools already write on your computer: nothing to set up, at no token cost.

On this channel: release videos, real sessions recorded from the app, and how-tos.

Website and download: https://agent-standup.com
Source code: https://github.com/Atoll92/agent-standup

Unofficial community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic, OpenAI or Mistral AI.
```
