# Agent Standup 1.32.0: talking points

Seven changes from the 1.32.0 changelog, each as the plain fact and then one line said in a standup.

## 1. Cowork sessions join the call
Sessions of Cowork, the tab of the Claude desktop app, now appear next to your Claude Code sessions: in the session list, on the board and in the live call, sub-agents included, with nothing to set up. Checked on macOS so far.
Cowork has been added to the standup invite. Attendance is optional. There is a switch.

## 2. Artifacts in the call
When a Claude Code session publishes an artifact, its author shares the real page on screen; a click pins it at full size, where you can scroll and use it until you close it. Nothing is fetched from claude.ai, and the page runs in a frame with no network access.
Someone shared their screen without asking whether we can all see it.

## 3. Natural voices by default, two voice styles
Voice style now offers two choices: the natural voices, the new default (one download of about 750 MB), and the Animal Crossing babble. The cartoon voices, Simlish and the plain system voices are gone.
Simlish has been moved to a different project. We wish it well.

## 4. Natural voices in four languages, and the call in Spanish or Portuguese
The natural voices now speak French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese as well as English. With Spanish or Brazilian Portuguese as the interface language, the call is held in that language; translating goes through your own Claude Code login, counts on your plan, and can be turned off.
The standup is now in Spanish. It still runs over, but it sounds better doing it.

## 5. The call follows the session you last touched, with a pin on a tab
The live call goes to the session you last started or typed in, no longer to whichever one wrote last. Click a tab to pin the call there; it stays until you ask something in another session or click the tab again.
The meeting now follows the person running it, not whoever types the loudest.

## 6. The Sessions view in sections
Sessions are grouped by what waits for you first: needs you?, your turn, working, waiting on sub-agents, idle, each with its count. A ✓ moves a finished session to a closed Done section at the bottom.
Finally, a status report with the blockers at the top.

## 7. Replays take turns
In a replay, sub-agents that spoke back to back are played one after another, in the order they spoke, as in the live call. Before, up to three voices played at once.
One person speaks at a time. Facilities has been asking for years.
