Agenda · seven items, fourteen minutes

  1. 00:00
    Cowork sessions join the call

    Sessions of Cowork, the tab of the Claude desktop app, appear next to your Claude Code sessions: in the list, on the board and in the live call.

    Cowork has been added to the standup invite. Attendance is optional. There is a switch.
  2. 00:02
    Artifacts in the call

    When a session publishes a page like this one, its author shares it on screen. A click pins it at full size. It runs in a frame with no network access.

    Someone shared their screen without asking whether we can all see it.
  3. 00:04
    Natural voices, two styles

    The natural voices are the default (one download of about 750 MB), next to the Animal Crossing babble. The cartoon voices, Simlish and the plain system voices are gone.

    Simlish has been moved to a different project. We wish it well.
  4. 00:06
    Four languages, and the call in Spanish or Portuguese

    The natural voices speak English, French, Spanish and Brazilian Portuguese. With a Spanish or Portuguese interface, the call is held in that language, through your own Claude Code; it can be turned off.

    The standup is now in Spanish. It still runs over, but it sounds better doing it.
  5. 00:08
    The call follows the session you last touched

    Start a session or type in one, and the call goes there. Click a tab to pin it: it stays until you ask something in another session.

    The meeting now follows the person running it, not whoever types the loudest.
  6. 00:10
    The Sessions view in sections

    What waits for you comes first, each section with its count. A ✓ puts a finished session away in Done.

    Finally, a status report with the blockers at the top.
  7. 00:12
    Replays take turns

    Sub-agents that spoke back to back now speak one after another, in the order they spoke.

    One person speaks at a time. Facilities has been asking for years.
