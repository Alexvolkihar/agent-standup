# Release video: on-screen captions

Announces release 1.32.0. The footage is the real call of the session that makes the video, held after 1.32.0 shipped: the release is done, so there is a meeting about it.
One caption at a time, at the bottom. Every caption is 52 characters or fewer. Tone: a relaxed office, dry, no exclamation marks.

## Hook

The caption of the first three seconds, over the start of the opening, before its first caption.

| # | English | Français |
|---|---------|----------|
| H1 | The release shipped. Naturally, there is a meeting | La version est sortie. Donc, forcément, une réunion |
| H2 | Your coding agents, as a live team call | Vos agents de code, en appel d’équipe en direct |
| H3 | Your agents shipped 1.32.0. Now they have a meeting | Vos agents ont sorti la 1.32.0. Place à la réunion |

Pick: **H2**. The first video's critic asked for what the app is before the joke, and a stranger still needs that first. The joke comes right after it, as the opening's caption, and returns at the recording.
